# How this was built, and why it works this way

> **DRAFT.** The author reviews every sentence before this is published.

I built this tracker for my own job search, then turned the parts that worked
into something anyone can set up in a few minutes. This page explains the choices, so you can judge whether they fit
you.

## The choices, and the reasons

- **It runs on your computer.** Your applications, answers and resume live
  in a folder you own. The setup page builds your download inside your
  browser and sends nothing anywhere. Claude reads your files with your own
  Claude account when you work with it.
- **What you want comes first.** Pay, balance, where you work, company size,
  how long you can search, and what you'd trade for your top priority. It's
  easy to take whatever comes along; a clear order makes offers easier to
  weigh.
- **A guide, not a form.** When an answer is vague, Claude asks one question
  at a time and says why. It doesn't praise empty answers or scold thin
  ones, and you can always skip.
- **Your words, not invented ones.** Everything you type goes into your files
  word for word. Drafts use only facts you've given; if a number is missing,
  Claude asks for it instead of making one up.
- **It never applies for you.** The job finder reads public listings and
  drafts; you decide and you send. It never logs in, submits, or creates
  accounts.
- **Work authorization is handled with care.** If you need sponsorship, the
  finder skips roles that rule you out and keeps your dates in view. It's
  general information, not legal advice, and your status never goes into a
  draft unless you ask.
- **Check-ins happen when something changes.** Your second session, an offer, a
  first final round, a run of rejections, a quiet stretch, or an approaching
  visa date. Not on a timer, and never more than once a week unless it matters.
- **The daily run is capped.** It uses your Claude usage within the run size
  you chose, and skips the day when enough is already waiting for your review.

## What it doesn't do

It doesn't guarantee interviews or offers, give legal advice, or share your
data with anyone. It's a tool for thinking clearly and staying organized;
the search is still yours.
